Version 3 artboards

Roadmap task r0. Four stages, each its own kind of place, one Claude Code chat everywhere, and side views that stay closed until you open them. Colours are the same tokens as today; pick a theme to check each one.

Top bar, 48 pxFour numbered stages with a progress line under each. A tick when a stage is done. Search and commands on the right.
Right railOverview, Live, GitHub and Chat as icons. Closed by default, a click opens one, Ctrl B hides it.
Status barBranch, changes, the running app, which agent, model, context and plan usage, in one quiet line.
Claude Code chatMonospace, > for you, ● for Claude, ⎿ for results, the turning star with a verb, prompts answered with 1 2 3.
Command paletteCtrl K holds everything that left the screen: agents, scans, decisions, notes, history, push.
Tabs, not splitsIn Development, Claude is the first tab. Files and terminals open as tabs beside it.
AutopilotOne click and Magnet takes over the stage: asks once, gives the orders, decides from the documents, reports in ASD-STE100.
Fewer boxesHairlines and sections instead of cards inside cards. Cards only where a thing floats or needs you.

Plan

A Claude chat that writes documents. The documents this project needs are listed on the right and ticked as they are written; one opens below to read or edit. Select text in a document to ask about it.

Design

Open full size

Like Claude Design. The brief on the left (references and where to find more, design system, components, images, colours, fonts), the screens on a canvas in the middle, the Designer on the right. Click an element to comment on it; the comment goes to the chat. Approve design writes the handoff.

03

Development

Open full size

Explorer on the left and one place in the middle with tabs, like VS Code. Claude is the first tab and stays pinned. Opening a file (Monaco editor, Claude's changes marked) or a terminal adds a tab next to it; switch tabs to go between chat, files and terminal. No split.

Deployment

Open full size

The cockpit. The running app in the middle, checks on the right, and Test, where Claude in Chrome walks through the test plan and shows each step with a screenshot. A failed step marks the spot on the preview and offers Fix in Development. Deploy asks once.

A command that needs you asks inside the chat, the way the CLI does: 1 Yes, 2 Yes and don't ask again, 3 No (Esc). Live shows the Builder's steps and background agents.

08

Autopilot: Magnet asks

Open full size

Click Autopilot in the top bar (Plan, Design or Development). Magnet asks "What should I do, sir?" and waits. Nothing runs until you answer. Answer once, then leave.

09

Autopilot: Magnet flies

Open full size

Magnet takes your seat. His orders to the Builder show as MAGNET → BUILDER. He answers the Builder from the documents, decides what they do not say and logs it, and checks each task's tests. The top bar shows him working; Esc stops him.

10

Autopilot: the report

Open full size

When you come back: a short report in ASD-STE100. Result, the decisions Magnet made (Keep or Change), what needs you (push is never done for you), and the next step.
